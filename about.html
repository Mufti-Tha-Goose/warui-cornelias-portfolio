MY STORY

Technology is the tool.
People are the point.

I'm Cornelias Kariuki Warui, an animator and full-stack developer based in Kenya. My work brings together web development, visual communication and creative problem-solving.

Over the years, I have worked on websites, digital products and communication projects across different sectors, including public services, non-profits, healthcare and creative campaigns. These experiences have taught me to look beyond the code: understand the problem, consider the people using the product, and make the solution clear and dependable.

I also enjoy writing, animation and visual storytelling. They influence how I approach technology — with curiosity, attention to detail and an interest in how people interact with information.
